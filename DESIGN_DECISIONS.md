## Identity / type thesis
This portfolio takes the form of a **human professional register** because her proven experience moves between precise court documentation and public-facing service; recruitment readers need to verify responsibilities quickly without mistaking her for a practicing lawyer.

## Specific design choices
1. **Portrait alongside record:** original submitted graduation image anchors an otherwise document-led layout. Alternative empty paper archive lacks humanity; photo placement evaluated at mobile widths.
2. **Ink / warm paper / restrained oxblood:** non-courtroom palette, not a hard-coded Lazarus brand. Alternative gold + marble + pillars would signal invented authority.
3. **Condensed display and human-scale body:** name fits three real-word lines; factual employer names preserve full form. Alternative oversized italic serif can look like a generic AI template.
4. **Real data rows, not invented case studies:** every expandable row shows sourced duties; capability tags mirror CV. Generic project cards rejected because no project samples are available.
5. **Filters communicate two actual work contexts:** Legal administration vs Service & operations; all remains default. They are reversible controls, not a scroll showpiece.
6. **Progress as an index mark:** moving horizontal rule indicates reading progress, not decorative motion. Reduced motion respected; no scroll hijacking.

## Anti-generic adversarial checks
- **Identity swap:** removing person name/photo still leaves the exact employer, court-administration duties and education. Pass as a design rationale, not independent perception research.
- **Skeleton:** title folio → contextual memo → expandable chronologically ordered register → credential sheet → correspondence; no cards-for-fake-projects, no SaaS feature comparison.
- **Rejected recurring defaults:** (a) centered massive serif + 2 CTAs; (b) 3-up identical rounded cards and fake KPI counters; (c) meaningless uniform reveal animations.
- **Blind perception:** NOT TESTED; requires independent participants.
